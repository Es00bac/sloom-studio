# Illustrated menu assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, 2026-09-26. Runtime originals are
`title-painting.png` and `control-atlas.png`. Typography is drawn by Qt.

The painting uses the previous menu capture as character/equipment reference,
with hand-painted gouache, ink contours, navy/teal architecture, warm ceramic
armor and amber practical light. Crew grouped on the right; calm negative space
on the left for controls. No rendered 3D screenshot or baked menu text.

The second painting corrects Axi's tail: remove the incorrect forward thigh
attachment, restore both separate armored legs, attach the tail behind her
pelvis at the base of her spine, trailing left behind her body. Preserve the
rest of the composition. The superseded image is kept on the art board in
validation/title/illustrated/.

The control atlas contains four painted industrial ceramic plates with matching
geometry for default, focus, primary and disabled states. It is sliced into real
native pixmaps, with labels and keyboard focus handled by the menu code.
