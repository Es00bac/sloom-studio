These places were built for people. The machines have reached another conclusion.

Qind Qompany works in inhabited industrial habitats: reclamation, rescue, and the unglamorous business of keeping a world livable. Their next job takes them into Tideglass Baths, a desalination and thermal spa complex of ceramic halls, warm pools, pump galleries and submerged service routes.

Six command sites anchor Tideglass: Arrival Atrium, Desalination Intake, Filtration Core, Lower Pumps, Salt Grotto and Heat Exchange. Holding and connecting those positions turns control of the habitat into new capability.

The Seedweb was manufactured to maintain such places. Its Custodian now treats independent occupants as faults. Cable beds creep across the floor. Ceramic deposits harden around reclaimed machinery. Inspection lenses turn toward the returning crew.

The Cyber Creep are the network’s working bodies: assembled tools, clamps, struts and fabrication hardware. Each destroyed body can be replaced. The habitat they are taking over cannot.

04 / Field guide

WIN THE ROOM.
CHANGE THE MAP.

The match ends when one side has no built command base left. A new weapon is useful. The infrastructure that makes it available is a target.

  1. 01

    Join a side

    Walk through a ready-room doorway for Qind Qompany, Cyber Creep, the smaller team, or spectating.

  2. 02

    Establish the front

    Follow field orders. Connect resource sockets and defend the structures that turn territory into supply.

  3. 03

    Expand & research

    Claim command sites, unlock tiers, and spend team and personal supply on the next advantage.

  4. 04

    Break their network

    Attack collectors, links, labs and bases. Keep your last built Outpost or Anchor standing.

On the ground

Your squad is the plan in motion.

Move with the team, cover the person building or welding, and read the commander’s waypoint. Return to a powered Outpost or Requisition to buy crew equipment; the hive uses an Anchor or Brood cache for fabrication. Patches holds G to stow his gun and work with the dedicated Arc shears welder. Releasing G draws the selected firearm again.

The crew are critically incapacitated at zero health. Linked medical teleportation retrieves and stabilizes them before a return pad redeploys ready personnel. Equipment is lost on incapacitation. The Creep mend near an Anchor, on their web after combat, or beside a Spool; their bodies are assembled again in waves.

In command

Your team is more than the fight you can see.

Face a nearby built command station and press E to take the chair. Select a field player to issue a targeted order, or issue a team destination with no selection. Place structures, manage research and keep the network connected.

Crew commanders can teleport in health and ammunition packs, spending the same team supply needed for expansion. Hive commanders invest in Seedweb growth, fabrication, Needles and trait chambers. Press E to return to the field.

Light, power and detection

The crew’s flashlights reveal useful ground and give their positions away. A damaged power junction drops a location to emergency lighting and stops crew stations and labs there. Hold E to restore it, then weld it. Creep sense reads structure, power and visible electrical signatures; separate hive sight reveals tagged or network-sensed crew through walls.

Water and body size

Deep water changes movement and visibility. CyberPengu, Ducké and Axi handle swimming well; other crew are slowed, and Hex scrambles out. Aim down while moving forward to dive. Bastion cannot use the small chassis’ vents or service crawls; a flank route can be an advantage even when it is too small for a heavy assault.

Supply, repair and reinforcement

Collectors and Siphons feed separate team and personal banks. Team supply pays for construction, research and crew support drops; personal supply pays for weapons, gear, chassis and hive traits. Crew medpacks restore health and welding restores armor. Stations provide ammunition, not free health. Protect the supply chain before asking it to support a push.

05 / Technology & infrastructure

BUILD A FRONT.
UNLOCK THE FUTURE.

Research proceeds in order: tier 1, then 2, then 3. Each needs a working station, time and team supply. On Tideglass, advanced technology also needs built bases. Losing a base can switch an already researched tier offline.

FOUNDATION

Outpost + power network

Collectors earn supply. Build a powered Research station to advance equipment tiers; add an Arms lab for Weapons and Armour upgrades.

An Arms lab runs one upgrade job at a time. A second lab lets the two lines run together. Each Weapons level adds 10% gun / melee damage; explosives and shears receive no damage bonus. Each Armour level adds 10 maximum armor. Destroy every working Arms lab and those researched bonuses go offline until a working lab returns.

FOUNDATION

Siphons earn supply. Looms extend territory. A powered Foundry Spire advances hive tiers; trait chambers turn the web into a specialized fighting network.

Kiln grants Carapace, Spur grants Celerity, and Veil grants Aura. A player buys each trait for 8 personal supply at a fabrication point and keeps it through chassis changes and new lives. Its strength follows the number of working chambers, capped by the active hive tier.

!

Hold the base that holds the technology.

Owned weapons, gear and chassis remain when a tier goes offline. New purchases and tier-gated abilities stop until the required bases return. Research is retained. Expansion creates capability; raiding infrastructure removes it.

Structure field manual

What every structure does.

Construction uses team supply. Costs below are the current Tideglass rules and may change during development.

Qind Qompany 07 structures

QC / 01Outpost65 supply

A command base, power root, reinforcement point and ammunition station. Build it on a free command-site socket; it takes 45 seconds while powered. The team must keep at least one completed Outpost standing to remain in the match.

Tier 3 needs two built Outposts.

QC / 02Collector10 supply

Claims an authored resource socket and generates team supply plus personal supply for every crew member while built and powered. Income stops if the Collector is destroyed or its power connection is cut.

QC / 03Relay5 supply

Extends the Outpost’s power network to further stations and resource sockets. A chain needs a live connection to an Outpost; isolated relays do not power each other indefinitely.

QC / 04Requisition20 supply

A forward equipment and ammunition station. Crew use a nearby working station to buy weapons and gear. It needs a power connection and a lit location; health recovery comes from medpacks, not the station.

QC / 05Research20 supply

Runs the three equipment-tier research jobs. It must be completed and powered, and the next tier’s base requirement must be met before research can begin. Each job spends team supply and takes time.

QC / 06Return gate20 supply

A low medical return pad that redeploys recovered personnel closer to the front. It needs power and a clear landing area. The team can route reinforcement through the forward pad; a base under siege can recall reinforcements home.

QC / 07Arms lab10 supply

Researches Weapons or Armour upgrades, one job per lab. A second lab can run the other line. The lab must be built, powered and in a lit location. With no working lab, the researched bonuses are retained but inactive.

Cyber Creep 10 structures

SW / 01Seed Anchor65 supply

The hive’s command base, Seedweb root, fabrication point, mending aura and reinforcement source. It occupies a free command site and takes 45 seconds to build. One, two and three built Anchors support hive tiers 1, 2 and 3.

Keep at least one completed Anchor standing.

SW / 02Salvage Siphon10 supply

Harvests a resource socket for the hive’s team and personal supply. A Siphon must be built and connected to the hive. Powered Siphons also raise the allowed number of Needle Arrays, to a maximum of three.

SW / 03Sentinel Loom5 supply

Extends connected Seedweb into new territory. A completed, connected Loom grows its patch along open routes, reaching through doorways rather than through walls. Destroying or disconnecting it can silence the machinery beyond it.

SW / 04Brood cache20 supply

A forward fabrication point where Creep spend personal supply to change chassis or buy available traits. It works while completed and connected; a protected cache keeps reassembly closer to the front.

SW / 05Foundry Spire20 supply

The hive’s research station. Runs tier research while completed and powered. Each successive tier needs the corresponding number of built Anchors, so a Spire cannot replace expansion.

SW / 06Transit Burrow20 supply

A connected reinforcement structure. The current rules prioritize built Anchors for hive reinforcement; a powered Burrow is a fallback return point when that selection has no suitable Anchor.

SW / 07Needle Array24 supply

A tier-1 emplacement that locks a visible crew member’s floor position, warns, charges and pulses into that circle. It forces movement and calls nearby hive bodies toward the marked target. Cover or leaving the circle avoids the pulse; cut its Loom, short it with shears, or interrupt its charge with the Ram.

Needs connected Seedweb, spacing from bases and resource sockets, and an available Siphon-supported capacity.

SW / 08Kiln15 supply

Raises the Carapace line: each working Kiln adds one level, capped by the hive’s active tier. Creep who bought Carapace gain extra armor equal to 5% of their chassis’s full health-and-armor pool per level.

SW / 09Spur15 supply

Raises the Celerity line. A purchased Celerity trait adds 4% movement pace per working level. A second or third Spur needs hive tier 2 or 3, respectively.

SW / 10Veil15 supply

Raises the Aura line. A body with the purchased trait contributes a hive-sight aura of four meters per working level, revealing crew in its range through walls to the network.

The habitat supplies the places to fight over.

Command sites are the authored sockets for an Outpost or Anchor. Only a base can occupy one; an unfinished base does not satisfy the survival or tier requirement.

Resource sockets take one Collector or Siphon. Both team and personal income depend on keeping those collectors built, alive and connected.

Power junctions govern a location’s lighting and crew station / lab operation. They are repairable habitat infrastructure, not a commander purchase.

06 / From the developer

TEAM-PLAY.
FIRST.

Caboose-TPF-Playing since NS 1.04

Natural Selection and Natural Selection 2 were a huge part of my formative years.

I played from NS 1.04 onward. On the old Unknown Worlds forums I went by Caboose-TPF-, with TPF standing for “team-play first.” I was deeply involved in that community, made custom maps, wrote thousands of forum posts, and briefly served as an internal tester for NS2.

I have a great deal of respect for Unknown Worlds and for the games and community they built. The shared decisions, the commander’s trust in the squad, and the way a good map could shape a whole match have stayed with me.

Qind Qompany comes from that love. It shares a genre and some familiar strategic ideas, while building its own world, characters, machine ecology and equipment. I think of that relationship in the wider lineage of games such as Gloom and the influence of the Alien franchise: recognizable inspirations, expressed through a distinct game.

A personal tribute to the joy of playing as a team.
