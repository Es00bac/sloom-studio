# Code of conduct

Sloom Studio is a small project maintained by one person and helped by volunteers. These rules
keep it a place people want to come back to.

- Be direct and be kind. Say what is wrong with the work, plainly, and leave the person out of it.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Disagreements about design go in the issue, with reasons, and end with a decision by the
  maintainer. You can disagree with the decision; you cannot relitigate it in every thread.
- No harassment, slurs, sexualised content or personal attacks, anywhere the project talks:
  GitHub, the forum, social accounts, email.
- Respect that contributors' time is unpaid. A request is a request, not a demand.
- Report problems to support@sloom.studio. The maintainer will read it, may ask questions, and
  will act, up to and including removing someone from the project's spaces.

The maintainer is bound by this too.
